Bibliotheca Tychoniana

The books and manuscripts Tycho Brahe is documented as having owned, where each surviving copy is today, and how the library was dispersed after his death. Every entry states its status, its grade and the source that attests it; a surviving volume gives the marks that identify it, its shelfmark and, where the holding library publishes them, its catalogue record and a digital facsimile.

72 volumes identified as Tycho’s survive, 70 of them in 14 institutions and 2 in private hands. The figure The descent of the surviving volumes (§9) traces the road each took from his library to its holder — the gifts of his lifetime, the heirs, the Jesuit houses — the Clementinum’s own gift of 1642 and the colleges whose libraries it gathered after 1773, the Swedish booty of 1648, private hands and the trade — and states the gaps in the record.
