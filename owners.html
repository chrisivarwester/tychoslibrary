Every person, house, firm and library through whose hands a surviving volume passed, from Tycho’s heirs to the holders today, in alphabetical order: the role, the dates the record gives, the volumes, and under each volume the basis of the step and its source. The names stand as the records give them — an owner not identified is listed as such, and the two American private collectors are two entries, not one. Where the Familia has the person, the name opens its record there.
