A retired record: vol-s-band-xxv

(Prandtl Verzeichnis B, Band XXV; shelfmark to capture) — a volume record

Retired on 5 June 2026. : duplicate of s-mort-rohsska-prachtband (Röhsska). Was mislabelled NKP/Prague; the object left Prague for Sweden (Per Hierta -> Röhsska). Merged into Zwinger, Theodor, Theatri humanae vitae volumen decimum-octavum [the last tome, volumina XVIII–XXIX: 33 books, pp. 3251–4373 and the index] (1587).

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
