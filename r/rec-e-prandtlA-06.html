A retired record: rec-e-prandtlA-06

Archimedes, Archimedis Syracusani opera

Retired on 18 October 2026. Prandtl’s Verzeichnis A no. 6 (Archimedes, Basel 1548, his Band IX) answers to no edition; it is read as a misdating of the Hervagius Archimedes of 1544 in Band VIII (Sign. 5 C 31), and no separate book is sought. Prusík 1901 (p. 200, group A no. 8) and Kukula 1906–07 (p. 23) dated 5 C 31 to 1548; Prandtl took his Band IX from Prusík and his Band VIII from Studnička, hence the double count (Sonderdruck pp. 11 and 14).

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
