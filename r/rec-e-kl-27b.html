A retired record: rec-e-kl-27b

Federico Commandino, Federici Commandini Urbinatis Liber de horologiorum descriptione

Retired on 18 October 2026. Commandino’s Liber de horologiorum descriptione is the second part of the Rome imprint of 1562 that carries Ptolemy’s De analemmate (Ptolemaeus, Claudius (ed. Federico Commandino), Liber de analemmate, a Federico Commandino instauratus; eiusdem Commandini Liber de horologiorum descriptione (1562), Kleinschnitzová 27:1); Šolc 2002 (p. 331) itemised it as a title, as he did the other parts of works retired on 18 October 2026. Neither Kleinschnitzová nor the library’s record lists it as a separate piece.

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.

Cite: Bibliotheca Tychoniana, Tychoniana, the retired record rec-e-kl-27b.
