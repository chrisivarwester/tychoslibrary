A retired record: rec-e-orphan-wroclaw

(unspecified), [Tychoniana reported at Wrocław University Library, Głombiowski 1955 — no title established]

Retired on 18 October 2026. The Wrocław ‘Tychonianum’ of Głombiowski 1955 is the Mechanica of 1598 that Tycho inscribed for Jakob Monau — an outgoing presentation copy carried in the census of that edition, not a book of the library.

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
