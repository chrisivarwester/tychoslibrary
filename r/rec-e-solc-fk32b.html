A retired record: rec-e-solc-fk32b

Pantaleon Candidus, De regibus Bohemicis libri quinque

Retired on 18 October 2026. Šolc 2002 (p. 331) lists ‘De regibus Bohemicis libri quinque’ as a second Candidus work in Sign. 9 B 135; it is the second part of the one Strasbourg imprint of 1587, ‘Bohemais, hoc est de ducibus Bohemicis libri duo, de regibus Bohemicis libri quinque’ (Kleinschnitzová 32:2), which the volume holds as Alma, Eilerdus de, Bellum giganteum (1588).

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
