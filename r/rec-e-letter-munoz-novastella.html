A retired record: rec-e-letter-munoz-novastella

Jerónimo Muñoz (Munosius), Libro del nuevo cometa / De nova stella (Spanish, Valencia 1573)

Retired on 18 October 2026. No document attests the book as Tycho’s: he asked Hagecius for Muñoz’s Spanish tract on the new star on 25 January 1590 (Opera omnia VII, 224); Hagecius answered on 1/11 June 1590 that he had his own copy, the author’s gift, and no one to translate it (VII, 251: ‘MUNNOSIJ libellum de nova stella Hispanico idiomate, ab ipsomet olim mihi missum et donatum, habeo, qui tamen eum Latinum faciat, habeo neminem’) and sent nothing; Tycho withdrew the request on 3 August 1590 (VII, 270), and the Progymnasmata say he never saw the text (III, 80). The store had read Hagecius’s letter as a gift.

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
