A retired record: rec-e-mort-rohsska-prachtband

(unidentified — a gilt-supralibros Prachtband), A Tycho gilt-supralibros Prachtband (title not identified)

Retired on 8 October 2026. The same volume as Prandtl A no. 57 / Band XXV (the Zwinger Theatrum humanae vitae XVIII at the Röhsska museum): Mortensen 1955 pp. 28–30 names the title, and the survivor s-mort-rohsska-prachtband already pointed to the Prandtl-numbered entry. The 'title not identified' record was a seed artefact that the merge of announced but never removed.

Its content is absorbed in the record Zwinger, Theodor, Theatri humanae vitae volumen decimum-octavum [the last tome, volumina XVIII–XXIX: 33 books, pp. 3251–4373 and the index] (1587).

Cite: Bibliotheca Tychoniana, Tychoniana, the retired record rec-e-mort-rohsska-prachtband.
