Paduanus, Joannes, Viridarium Mathematicorum

Retired on 18 October 2026. Prandtl’s A 26 ‘Joa. Paduani opera, Venetiis 1563’ (Band XIV, after Prusík 1901, p. 200) and A 27 ‘Viridarium Mathematicorum’ (Band XV, after Studnička 1901, p. 54) are one book, the Viridarium mathematicorum of 1563 at Sign. 14 H 190 (Kleinschnitzová no. 15; the library’s record); merged into Paduanus, Joannes, Viridarium mathematicorum (1563).

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
