A retired record: rec-e-solc-fk21b

Brevis explicatio doctrinae sinuum (followed by tables)

Retired on 18 October 2026. Šolc 2002 (p. 330) lists ‘Brevis explicatio doctrinae sinuum followed by tables’ among the texts of Sign. 14 K 70; it is the sinus doctrine of Brucaeus’s De motu primo libri tres (Rostock 1573), the volume’s second print (Brucaeus, Henricus, Henrici Brucaei de motu primo libri tres (1573)) — a part of a work.

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
