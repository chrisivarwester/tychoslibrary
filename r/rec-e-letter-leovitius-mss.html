A retired record: rec-e-letter-leovitius-mss

Leovitius, Cyprianus, Manuscripts at the Fugger Library: Ephemerides; Tabulae directionum resolutae; the great De genethliaca astrologia

Retired on 20 October 2026. A loan, not an acquisition: Herwart borrowed the Fugger manuscripts for Tycho and the entry's own note says he never owned them (Thoren, p. 421 n. 205); retired by the rule that retired the Braun Theatrum (a documented loan is not an entry).

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
