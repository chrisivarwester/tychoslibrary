A retired record: rec-e-letter-scaliger-praecessione

Scaliger, Joseph Justus, Diatriba de praecessione aequinoctiorum

Retired on 20 October 2026. Never a book in his hands: Scaliger's letter of 10 March 1600 says the Diatriba is still in draft and that he intends to send it; nothing was printed before 1613 (Dreyer, Opera omnia VIII, p. 443), and no receipt is documented — the entry rested on a stated intention.

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
