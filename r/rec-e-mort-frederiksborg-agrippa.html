A retired record: rec-e-mort-frederiksborg-agrippa

Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa von Nettesheim, Opera, tomus I (Lyon, ca. 1550, 8vo)

Retired on 28 September 2026. The same volume as Prandtl A no. 48 / Band XX (Prandtl 1932 lists Band XX with Mortensen); provenance and notes folded into the Prandtl-numbered entry.

Its content is absorbed in the record Agrippa von Nettesheim, Heinrich Cornelius, Opera, tomus primus (c. 1550).
