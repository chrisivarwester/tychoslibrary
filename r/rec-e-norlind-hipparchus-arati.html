A retired record: rec-e-norlind-hipparchus-arati

Hipparchus (ed. princeps, Petrus Victorius), In Arati et Eudoxi Phaenomena libri III

Retired on 18 October 2026. The same object as Hipparchus (ed. Petrus Victorius), the copy annotated by Joseph Justus Scaliger, In Arati et Eudoxi Phaenomena libri III (the Greek editio princeps, with Scaliger’s annotations) (1567) — the Victorius Hipparchus of 1567 with Scaliger’s annotations, requested in August 1598 (Opera omnia VIII, 102), acknowledged in March 1599 (VIII, 151) and still with the heirs when Kepler asked Jørgen Brahe for it in November 1624 (KGW XIX, no. 7) — entered twice; Norlind’s record and the Ripensis story move to the surviving entry.

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
