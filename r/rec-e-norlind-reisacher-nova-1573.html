Note
Norlind 1970, p. 361 (Zinner 2647), lists the tract among the books of the library because Tycho discusses it in the Progymnasmata (Opera omnia III, 45–48 and III, 259–262); no copy, receipt or mark is recorded. Norlind’s introduction states the principle on which such tracts enter his catalogue: it may be regarded as fairly certain that nearly all the writings on the new star of 1572 and the comet of 1577 that Tycho reviews in the Progymnasmata and the comet book stood in the library on Hven (p. 335). As an unbound tract, Tycho’s copy would carry no known mark by which it could be recognised, and none has been identified.
