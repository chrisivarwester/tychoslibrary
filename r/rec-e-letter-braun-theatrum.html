A retired record: rec-e-letter-braun-theatrum

Georg Braun & Frans Hogenberg, Theatrum urbium / Civitates orbis terrarum (the city-atlas; sought the further volumes)

Retired on 18 October 2026. The fourth part of the Civitates orbis terrarum that was at Hven in December 1588 was Rantzau’s loan (Norlind 1926, p. 218, among the loans; Opera omnia VII, 386; Rantzau’s own copy is at Augsburg, Gebele 1926), and Norlind found no evidence that Tycho owned parts I–III; a borrowed book is outside this catalogue.

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
