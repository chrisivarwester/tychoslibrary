A retired record: rec-e-solc-fk15b

Antimachus (dialogue)

Retired on 18 October 2026. Šolc 2002 (p. 330) lists ‘Antimachus’ and ‘Nicomedes’ as two dialogues following the prints in Sign. 14 H 190; they are the interlocutors of Maurolico’s Cosmographia in tres dialogos (Venice 1543), the volume’s second print (Maurolycus, Franciscus, Cosmographia (1543)) — parts of a work, not works.

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
