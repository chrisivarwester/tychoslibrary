A retired record: vol-s-band-xx

(Prandtl Verzeichnis B, Band XX; shelfmark not recorded) — a volume record

Retired on 28 September 2026. Prandtl 1932 lists Band XX with Harald Mortensen at Copenhagen, not at the Clementinum; it is the Frederiksborg Agrippa (inventory B3950a-b), already carried as its own record.

Its content is absorbed in the volume record B3950a-b.
