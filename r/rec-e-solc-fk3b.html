A retired record: rec-e-solc-fk3b

Heinrich Rantzau (Henricus Ranzovius), Astrologicae quaedam praedictiones; adiectus est tractatus De annis climactericis

Retired on 18 October 2026. Šolc 2002 (p. 330) lists ‘Astrologicae quaedam praedictiones; adiectus est tractatus De annis climactericis’ as a second Rantzau item in Sign. 1 H 56; it is the continuation of the title of the one Leipzig print of 1584 (Rantzau, Heinrich (Ranzovius), Henrici Ranzovii catalogus imperatorum regum ac virorum illustrium qui artem astrologicam amarunt, ornarunt et exercuerunt (1584)).

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
