A retired record: rec-e-prandtlA-38

Reinhold, Erasmus, Initium Canonum Prutenicorum

Retired on 18 October 2026. Prandtl’s A 38 (Initium canonum Prutenicorum) and A 39 (Logistice scrupulorum), his Band XI, are the section titles of the second, title-less copy of Reinhold’s Prutenicae tabulae at Sign. 14 J 192 (Prusík 1901, p. 199, group A no. 5; Kleinschnitzová 1933, no. 20, and under no. 18: ‘Brahe besass das Werk noch in einem zweiten Exemplare’), one work, carried as Reinhold, Erasmus, Prutenicae tabulae coelestium motuum (the Initium canonum Prutenicorum and the Logistice scrupulorum astronomicorum, the copy without its title) (1551 or 1562); the edition had merged Band XI into the Mansfeld copy 14 J 176.

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
