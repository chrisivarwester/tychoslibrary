A retired record: rec-e-ghost-albategnius-arabic

al-Battani (Albategnius), De motu stellarum — a corrected/Arabic edition Tycho sought (1598-1600)

Retired on 18 October 2026. A better, Arabic text of al-Battani that Tycho sought in 1598–1600 and is not documented as having received; no printed Arabic al-Battani existed. The Nuremberg edition of 1537 he owned is Alfraganus, Rudimenta astronomica Alfragani. Item Albategnius astronomus peritissimus de motu stellarum ....

The register of retired entries names the entries that stand for it.
