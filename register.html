The full records, in alphabetical order of author (anonymous works under their title), numbered continuously for citation in this chapter. Each record gives status and grade, imprint, the attesting sources with their numbers, the ownership marks and annotations recorded, the surviving copy with its links, the road of dispersal where one is documented, the editorial note, and, where Norlind catalogued the work, a transcription of his entry. The record key printed with each entry is stable across versions and is the form to cite; the running number may change when entries are added.
