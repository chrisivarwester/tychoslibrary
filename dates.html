Enter a span of years to see what in the library is dated within it: the volumes whose binding carries a year, the inscriptions that carry one, what the sources otherwise date to those years, and the works printed in them. Each row gives the words of the record it is taken from and leads to that record; nothing is dated here that the record does not date. A year a record names for another reason, such as the year of a book cited, of a comparison or of the literature, is left out.

Without a span the section lists every dated item, earliest first: 24 dated bindings, 59 dated inscriptions, 161 items of other evidence and 206 imprints. The inscriptions include those of the houses that held the books after Tycho’s death; they date the volume’s passage, not its acquisition. A binding of a dated type is listed under the whole span of that type.
